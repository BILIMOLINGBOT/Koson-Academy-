“To be” fe'lining so'roq va bo'lishli shakli

So'roq shakli (Question)

1. Holatni so'rash (State)
Am I tired? — Men charchaganmanmi? Is she happy? — U xursandmi?

2. Kasb yoki maqom (Job / Status)
Is he a teacher? — U o‘qituvchimi? Am I a student? — Men talabamanmi?

3. Yoshi (Age)
Am I 20 years old? — Men 20 yoshdamanmi? Is he 30 years old? — U 30 yoshdami?

4. Qayerda ekanini so'rash (Place)
Am I at home? — Men uydamanmi? Are they in Tashkent? — Ular Toshkentdami?

5. Sifat bilan so'roq (Adjective)
Is the weather cold? — Ob-havo sovuqmi? Is my phone new? — Mening telefonim yangimi?

Bo'lishli shakl (Positive)

1. Holatni bildirish (State)
I am tired. — Men charchaganman. She is happy. — U xursand.

2. Kasb yoki maqom (Job / Status)
He is a teacher. — U o‘qituvchi. I am a student. — Men talabaman.

3. Yoshi (Age)
I am 20 years old. — Men 20 yoshdaman. He is 30 years old. — U 30 yoshda.

4. Qayerda ekanini aytish (Place)
I am at home. — Men uydaman. They are in Tashkent. — Ular Toshkentda.

5. Sifat bilan gap (Adjective)
The weather is cold. — Ob-havo sovuq. My phone is new. — Mening telefonim yangi.
