Future Simple — So'roq shakl (Question)

1. Kelajakda sodir bo'ladigan ish-harakat (Future action)
Will I visit my grandmother tomorrow? — Men ertaga buvimni ziyorat qilamanmi? Will she cook dinner tonight? — U bugun kechqurun kechki ovqat pishiradimi? Will they play football next week? — Ular keyingi hafta futbol o'ynaydimi?

2. Kelajakdagi holat (Future state)
Will he work at a bank? — U bankda ishlaydimi? Will we live in Tashkent? — Biz Toshkentda yashaymizmi? Will I study English? — Men ingliz tilini o'rganamanmi?

3. Bashorat yoki taxmin (Prediction)
Will it rain tomorrow? — Ertaga yomg'ir yog'adimi? Will the team win the match? — Jamoa o'yinda g'alaba qozonadimi? Will she be a great doctor? — U ajoyib shifokor bo'ladimi?

4. O'sha paytda qabul qilingan qaror (Spontaneous decision)
Will I help you with that? — Men sizga bunda yordam beramanmi? Will I answer the phone? — Men telefonga javob beramanmi? Will we take a taxi? — Biz taksi olamizmi?

5. Va'da yoki taklif (Promise or offer)
Will I call you tomorrow? — Men sizga ertaga qo'ng'iroq qilamanmi? Will she help you with your homework? — U sizga uy vazifangizda yordam beradimi? Will they come to the party? — Ular ziyofatga kelishadimi?
