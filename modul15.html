Past Continuous — Inkor shakl (Negative)

1. O'tgan paytda davom etmagan ish-harakat (Action not in progress in the past)
I was not reading a book. — Men kitob o'qiyotgan emas edim. She was not cooking dinner. — U kechki ovqat pishirayotgan emas edi. They were not playing football. — Ular futbol o'ynayotgan emas edi.

2. O'tgan paytdagi davomli holat (Continuous state in the past)
He was not working at a bank. — U bankda ishlayotgan emas edi. We were not living in Tashkent. — Biz Toshkentda yashayotgan emas edik. I was not studying English. — Men ingliz tilini o'rganayotgan emas edim.

3. Bir ish davom etayotganda boshqa ish sodir bo'ldi (Interrupted action)
I was not sleeping when the phone rang. — Telefon jiringlaganda men uxlayotgan emas edim. She was not walking when it started to rain. — Yomg'ir boshlanganda u yurgan emas edi. They were not eating when I arrived. — Men kelganimda ular ovqat yeyayotgan emas edi.

4. Bir vaqtda davom etgan ikki ish (Parallel actions)
I was not cooking while she was cleaning. — Men pishirayotgan emas edim, u tozalayotgan edi. He was not reading while they were watching TV. — U o'qiyotgan emas edi, ular televizor ko'rayotgan edi. We were not talking while the children were playing. — Biz gaplashayotgan emas edik, bolalar o'ynayotgan edi.

5. O'tgan paytdagi vaqtinchalik holat (Temporary situation in the past)
He was not staying at a hotel. — U mehmonxonada turgan emas edi. I was not taking a course last month. — Men o'tgan oy kurs o'qiyotgan emas edim. She was not working from home. — U uydan ishlayotgan emas edi.
