"Was" va "Were" — So'roq shakl (Question)

1. Holatni so'rash (State)
Was I tired? — Men charchagan edimmi? Was she happy? — U xursand edimi? Were they ready? — Ular tayyor edimi?

2. Kasb yoki maqom (Job / Status)
Was he a teacher? — U o'qituvchi edimi? Was I a student? — Men talaba edimmi? Were we doctors? — Biz shifokor edikmi?

3. Yoshi (Age)
Was I 20 years old? — Men 20 yoshda edimmi? Was she 15 years old? — U 15 yoshda edimi? Were they children? — Ular bola edimi?

4. Qayerda ekanini so'rash (Place)
Was I at home? — Men uyda edimmi? Was he at work? — U ishda edimi? Were we in Tashkent? — Biz Toshkentda edikmi?

5. Sifat bilan so'roq (Adjective)
Was the weather cold? — Ob-havo sovuq edimi? Was my phone new? — Mening telefonim yangi edimi? Was the movie interesting? — Kino qiziqarli edimi?

6. Vaqt va sana (Time & Date)
Was it Monday? — Dushanba edimi? Was it 9 o'clock? — Soat 9 edimi? Was it summer? — Yoz edimi?
