"Was" va "Were" — Bo'lishli shakl (Positive)

1. Holatni bildirish (State)
I was tired yesterday. — Men kecha charchagan edim. She was happy. — U xursand edi. They were ready. — Ular tayyor edi.

2. Kasb yoki maqom (Job / Status)
He was a teacher. — U o'qituvchi edi. I was a student. — Men talaba edim. We were doctors. — Biz shifokor edik.

3. Yoshi (Age)
I was 20 years old. — Men 20 yoshda edim. She was 15 years old. — U 15 yoshda edi. They were children. — Ular bola edi.

4. Qayerda ekanini aytish (Place)
I was at home. — Men uyda edim. He was at work. — U ishda edi. We were in Tashkent. — Biz Toshkentda edik.

5. Sifat bilan gap (Adjective)
The weather was cold. — Ob-havo sovuq edi. My phone was new. — Mening telefonim yangi edi. The movie was interesting. — Kino qiziqarli edi.

6. Vaqt va sana (Time & Date)
It was Monday. — Dushanba edi. It was 9 o'clock. — Soat 9 edi. It was summer. — Yoz edi.
