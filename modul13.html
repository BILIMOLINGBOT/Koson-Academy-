Past Continuous — Bo'lishli shakl (Positive)

1. O'tgan paytda davom etayotgan ish-harakat (Action in progress in the past)
I was reading a book. — Men kitob o'qiyotgan edim. She was cooking dinner. — U kechki ovqat pishirayotgan edi. They were playing football. — Ular futbol o'ynayotgan edi.

2. O'tgan paytdagi davomli holat (Continuous state in the past)
He was working at a bank. — U bankda ishlayotgan edi. We were living in Tashkent. — Biz Toshkentda yashayotgan edik. I was studying English. — Men ingliz tilini o'rganayotgan edim.

3. Bir ish davom etayotganda boshqa ish sodir bo'ldi (Interrupted action)
I was sleeping when the phone rang. — Telefon jiringlaganda men uxlayotgan edim. She was walking when it started to rain. — Yomg'ir boshlanganda u yurgan edi. They were eating when I arrived. — Men kelganimda ular ovqat yeyayotgan edi.

4. Bir vaqtda davom etgan ikki ish (Parallel actions)
I was cooking while she was cleaning. — Men pishirayotgan edim, u tozalayotgan edi. He was reading while they were watching TV. — U o'qiyotgan edi, ular televizor ko'rayotgan edi. We were talking while the children were playing. — Biz gaplashayotgan edik, bolalar o'ynayotgan edi.

5. O'tgan paytdagi vaqtinchalik holat (Temporary situation in the past)
He was staying at a hotel. — U mehmonxonada turgan edi. I was taking a course last month. — Men o'tgan oy kurs o'qiyotgan edim. She was working from home. — U uydan ishlayotgan edi.
