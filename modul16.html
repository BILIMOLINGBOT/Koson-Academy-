Future Continuous — Bo'lishli shakl (Positive)

1. Kelajakda ma'lum paytda davom etayotgan ish (Action in progress at a specific time in the future)
I will be reading a book at 8 PM. — Men soat 8 da kitob o'qiyotgan bo'laman. She will be cooking dinner tomorrow. — U ertaga kechki ovqat pishirayotgan bo'ladi. They will be playing football this time next week. — Keyingi hafta shu paytda ular futbol o'ynayotgan bo'ladi.

2. Kelajakda davom etadigan holat (Continuous state in the future)
He will be working at a bank next year. — U keyingi yil bankda ishlayotgan bo'ladi. We will be living in Tashkent next month. — Biz keyingi oy Toshkentda yashayotgan bo'lamiz. I will be studying English this evening. — Men bugun kechqurun ingliz tilini o'rganayotgan bo'laman.

3. Kelajakda davom etayotgan vaqtinchalik holat (Temporary situation in the future)
He will be staying at a hotel next week. — U keyingi hafta mehmonxonada turgan bo'ladi. I will be taking a course this summer. — Men bu yoz kurs o'qiyotgan bo'laman. She will be working from home tomorrow. — U ertaga uydan ishlayotgan bo'ladi.

4. Kelajakdagi rejalashtirilgan davomli ish (Planned continuous action)
We will be traveling to Paris next month. — Biz keyingi oy Parijga sayohat qilayotgan bo'lamiz. They will be having a meeting at 10 AM. — Ular soat 10 da yig'ilish o'tkazayotgan bo'ladi. I will be waiting for you at the station. — Men sizni stansiyada kutayotgan bo'laman.

5. Odatiy kelajakdagi davomli ish (Routine continuous action in the future)
I will be working all day tomorrow. — Men ertaga kun bo'yi ishlayotgan bo'laman. She will be studying for the exam next week. — U keyingi hafta imtihonga tayyorlanayotgan bo'ladi. They will be building a new school. — Ular yangi maktab qurayotgan bo'ladi.
