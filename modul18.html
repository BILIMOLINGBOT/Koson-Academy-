Future Continuous — Inkor shakl (Negative)

1. Kelajakda ma'lum paytda davom etmayotgan ish (Action not in progress at a specific time in the future)
I will not be reading a book at 8 PM. — Men soat 8 da kitob o'qiyotgan bo'lmayman. She will not be cooking dinner tomorrow. — U ertaga kechki ovqat pishirayotgan bo'lmaydi. They will not be playing football this time next week. — Keyingi hafta shu paytda ular futbol o'ynayotgan bo'lmaydi.

2. Kelajakda davom etmaydigan holat (Continuous state in the future)
He will not be working at a bank next year. — U keyingi yil bankda ishlayotgan bo'lmaydi. We will not be living in Tashkent next month. — Biz keyingi oy Toshkentda yashayotgan bo'lmaymiz. I will not be studying English this evening. — Men bugun kechqurun ingliz tilini o'rganayotgan bo'lmayman.

3. Kelajakda davom etmayotgan vaqtinchalik holat (Temporary situation in the future)
He will not be staying at a hotel next week. — U keyingi hafta mehmonxonada turgan bo'lmaydi. I will not be taking a course this summer. — Men bu yoz kurs o'qiyotgan bo'lmayman. She will not be working from home tomorrow. — U ertaga uydan ishlayotgan bo'lmaydi.

4. Kelajakdagi rejalashtirilmagan davomli ish (Planned continuous action)
We will not be traveling to Paris next month. — Biz keyingi oy Parijga sayohat qilayotgan bo'lmaymiz. They will not be having a meeting at 10 AM. — Ular soat 10 da yig'ilish o'tkazayotgan bo'lmaydi. I will not be waiting for you at the station. — Men sizni stansiyada kutayotgan bo'lmayman.

5. Odatiy kelajakdagi davomli ish (Routine continuous action in the future)
I will not be working all day tomorrow. — Men ertaga kun bo'yi ishlayotgan bo'lmayman. She will not be studying for the exam next week. — U keyingi hafta imtihonga tayyorlanayotgan bo'lmaydi. They will not be building a new school. — Ular yangi maktab qurayotgan bo'lmaydi.
