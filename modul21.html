Present Simple — Inkor shakl (Negative)

1. Doimiy bajarilmaydigan ish-harakat (Habitual action)
I do not wake up at 7 AM. — Men soat 7 da uyg'onmayman. She does not drink coffee. — U kofe ichmaydi. They do not play football on Sundays. — Ular yakshanba kunlari futbol o'ynamaydi.

2. Umumiy haqiqat (General truth)
The sun does not rise in the west. — Quyosh g'arbdan chiqmaydi. Water does not boil at 50 degrees. — Suv 50 darajada qaynamaydi. The Earth does not go around the Moon. — Yer Oy atrofida aylanmaydi.

3. Doimiy holat (Permanent state)
I do not live in London. — Men Londonda yashamayman. He does not work at a hospital. — U shifoxonada ishlamaydi. She does not have any sisters. — Uning opa-singillari yo'q.

4. Reja yoki jadval (Schedule / Timetable)
The train does not leave at 10 AM. — Poyezd soat 10 da jo'namaydi. The lesson does not start at 9 o'clock. — Dars soat 9 da boshlanmaydi. The shop does not open on Sundays. — Do'kon yakshanba kunlari ochilmaydi.

5. Fakt yoki fikr (Fact or opinion)
I do not like coffee. — Men kofeni yaxshi ko'rmayman. She does not think English is difficult. — U ingliz tilini qiyin deb o'ylamaydi. They do not know the answer. — Ular javobni bilishmaydi.
