Present Continuous — Inkor shakl (Negative)

1. Hozir bajarilmayotgan ish-harakat (Action not happening now)
I am not reading a book. — Men kitob o'qimayapman. She is not cooking dinner. — U kechki ovqat pishirmayapti. They are not playing football. — Ular futbol o'ynamayapti.

2. Hozirgi paytdagi holat (Current situation)
He is not working at a bank. — U bankda ishlamayapti. We are not living in Tashkent. — Biz Toshkentda yashamayapmiz. I am not studying English. — Men ingliz tilini o'rganmayapman.

3. Rejalashtirilgan kelajak (Planned future)
I am not meeting my friend tomorrow. — Men ertaga do'stim bilan uchrashmayapman. She is not traveling to Paris next week. — U keyingi hafta Parijga ketmayapti. We are not having a party on Saturday. — Biz shanba kuni ziyofat o'tkazmayapmiz.

4. Vaqtinchalik holat (Temporary situation)
He is not staying at a hotel. — U mehmonxonada turmayapti. I am not taking a course this month. — Men bu oy kurs o'qimayapman. She is not working from home today. — U bugun uydan ishlamayapti.

5. O'zgarayotgan vaziyat (Changing situation)
The weather is not getting warmer. — Ob-havo iliqlashmayapti. Prices are not increasing. — Narxlar oshmayapti. My English is not improving. — Mening ingliz tilim yaxshilanmayapti.
