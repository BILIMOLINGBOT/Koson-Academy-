Present Simple — Bo'lishli shakl (Positive)

1. Doimiy bajariladigan ish-harakat (Habitual action)
I wake up at 7 AM every day. — Men har kuni soat 7 da uyg'onaman. She drinks coffee every morning. — U har kuni ertalab kofe ichadi. They play football on Sundays. — Ular yakshanba kunlari futbol o'ynaydi.

2. Umumiy haqiqat (General truth)
The sun rises in the east. — Quyosh sharqdan chiqadi. Water boils at 100 degrees. — Suv 100 darajada qaynaydi. The Earth goes around the Sun. — Yer Quyosh atrofida aylanadi.

3. Doimiy holat (Permanent state)
I live in Tashkent. — Men Toshkentda yashayman. He works at a bank. — U bankda ishlaydi. She has two brothers. — Uning ikkita akasi bor.

4. Reja yoki jadval (Schedule / Timetable)
The train leaves at 9 AM. — Poyezd soat 9 da jo'naydi. The lesson starts at 8 o'clock. — Dars soat 8 da boshlanadi. The shop opens at 10 AM. — Do'kon soat 10 da ochiladi.

5. Fakt yoki fikr (Fact or opinion)
I like apples. — Men olmani yaxshi ko'raman. She thinks English is easy. — U ingliz tilini oson deb o'ylaydi. They know the answer. — Ular javobni bilishadi.
