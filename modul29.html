"Have got" va "Have" — So'roq shakl (Question)

1. Oil a'zolari (Family members)
Have I got a brother? — Mening akam bormi? Has she got two sisters? — Uning ikkita opasi bormi? Do I have a big family? — Mening katta oilam bormi?

2. Shaxsiy narsalar (Personal possessions)
Have I got a new phone? — Mening yangi telefonim bormi? Has he got a car? — Uning mashinasi bormi? Do we have a house in Tashkent? — Bizning Toshkentda uyimiz bormi?

3. Tashqi ko'rinish (Physical appearance)
Has she got long hair? — Uning sochlari uzunmi? Has he got blue eyes? — Uning ko'zlari ko'kmi? Do I have short hair? — Mening sochlarim kaltami?

4. Kasallik yoki holat (Illness / Condition)
Have I got a headache? — Mening boshim og'riyaptimi? Has she got a cold? — U shamallaganmi? Does he have a fever? — Uning isitmasi bormi?

5. Imkoniyat yoki reja (Opportunity / Plan)
Have I got a meeting today? — Bugun mening uchrashuvim bormi? Have we got tickets for the concert? — Bizda konsertga chiptalar bormi? Does she have an exam tomorrow? — Uning ertaga imtihoni bormi?

6. Hayvonlar (Animals / Pets)
Have I got a cat? — Mening mushugim bormi? Have they got a dog? — Ularning iti bormi? Does he have a parrot? — Uning to'tisi bormi?
