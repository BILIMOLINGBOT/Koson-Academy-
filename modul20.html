Present Simple — So'roq shakl (Question)

1. Doimiy bajariladigan ish-harakat (Habitual action)
Do I wake up at 7 AM every day? — Men har kuni soat 7 da uyg'onamanmi? Does she drink coffee every morning? — U har kuni ertalab kofe ichadimi? Do they play football on Sundays? — Ular yakshanba kunlari futbol o'ynaydimi?

2. Umumiy haqiqat (General truth)
Does the sun rise in the east? — Quyosh sharqdan chiqadimi? Does water boil at 100 degrees? — Suv 100 darajada qaynaydimi? Does the Earth go around the Sun? — Yer Quyosh atrofida aylanadimi?

3. Doimiy holat (Permanent state)
Do I live in Tashkent? — Men Toshkentda yashaymanmi? Does he work at a bank? — U bankda ishlaydimi? Does she have two brothers? — Uning ikkita akasi bormi?

4. Reja yoki jadval (Schedule / Timetable)
Does the train leave at 9 AM? — Poyezd soat 9 da jo'naydimi? Does the lesson start at 8 o'clock? — Dars soat 8 da boshlanadimi? Does the shop open at 10 AM? — Do'kon soat 10 da ochiladimi?

5. Fakt yoki fikr (Fact or opinion)
Do I like apples? — Men olmani yaxshi ko'ramanmi? Does she think English is easy? — U ingliz tilini oson deb o'ylaydimi? Do they know the answer? — Ular javobni bilishadimi?
