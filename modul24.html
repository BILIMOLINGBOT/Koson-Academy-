Past Simple — Inkor shakl (Negative)

1. O'tgan zamonda bajarilmagan ish-harakat (Completed action in the past)
I did not visit my grandmother yesterday. — Men kecha buvimni ziyorat qilmadim. She did not cook dinner last night. — U kecha kechki ovqat pishirmadi. They did not play football last week. — Ular o'tgan hafta futbol o'ynamadi.

2. O'tgan zamondagi holat (State in the past)
He did not work at a bank. — U bankda ishlamagan. We did not live in Tashkent. — Biz Toshkentda yashamaganmiz. I did not study English at school. — Men maktabda ingliz tilini o'rganmaganman.

3. Ketma-ket sodir bo'lmagan ish-harakatlar (Sequence of actions)
I did not wake up, get dressed, or leave the house. — Men uyg'onmadim, kiyinmadim va uydan chiqmadim. She did not finish her homework or watch TV. — U uy vazifasini tugatmadi va televizor ko'rmadi. They did not arrive, sit down, or order food. — Ular kelishmadi, o'tirishmadi va ovqat buyurtma qilishmadi.

4. O'tgan zamondagi odat (Habit in the past)
I did not walk to school every day. — Men har kuni maktabga piyoda bormasdim. She did not drink tea every morning. — U har kuni ertalab choy ichmasdi. They did not visit us often. — Ular bizga tez-tez tashrif buyurishmasdi.

5. Aniq vaqtda sodir bo'lmagan ish (Action at a specific time)
I did not meet him last Monday. — Men u bilan o'tgan dushanba kuni uchrashmadim. She did not call me at 5 PM. — U menga soat 5 da qo'ng'iroq qilmadi. They did not move here in 2020. — Ular bu yerga 2020-yilda ko'chib kelishmadi.
