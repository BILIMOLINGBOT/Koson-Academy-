Ingliz tilida "To be" fe'lining 5 ta asosiy holati

1. Ism va kasb-hunarni aytishda

I am a student.
Men talabaman.
He is an engineer.
U muhandis.
They are from Uzbekistan.
Ular O'zbekistondan.

2. Yoshni bildirishda

She is 20 years old.
U 20 yoshda.
I am 18.
Men 18 daman.
My brother is 10.
Mening ukam 10 yoshda.

3. Sifat, xususiyat yoki his-tuyg'ularni tasvirlashda

You are very kind.
Siz juda mehribonsiz.
It is a beautiful car.
Bu chiroyli mashina.
We are hungry.
Biz ochmiz / qornimiz ochdi.

4. Joylashuvni (qayerdaligini) aytishda

I am at work.
Men ishdaman.
The keys are on the table.
Kalitlar stolning ustida.
She is in London.
U Londonda.

5. Ob-havo, vaqt va narx-navoni aytishda

It is 9 o'clock.
Soat 9 bo'ldi.
It is hot today.
Bugun kun issiq.
This shirt is 25 dollars.
Bu ko'ylak 25 dollar turadi.
