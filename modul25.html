Future Simple — Bo'lishli shakl (Positive)

1. Kelajakda sodir bo'ladigan ish-harakat (Future action)
I will visit my grandmother tomorrow. — Men ertaga buvimni ziyorat qilaman. She will cook dinner tonight. — U bugun kechqurun kechki ovqat pishiradi. They will play football next week. — Ular keyingi hafta futbol o'ynaydi.

2. Kelajakdagi holat (Future state)
He will work at a bank. — U bankda ishlaydi. We will live in Tashkent. — Biz Toshkentda yashaymiz. I will study English. — Men ingliz tilini o'rganaman.

3. Bashorat yoki taxmin (Prediction)
It will rain tomorrow. — Ertaga yomg'ir yog'adi. The team will win the match. — Jamoa o'yinda g'alaba qozonadi. She will be a great doctor. — U ajoyib shifokor bo'ladi.

4. O'sha paytda qabul qilingan qaror (Spontaneous decision)
I will help you with that. — Men sizga bunda yordam beraman. I will answer the phone. — Men telefonga javob beraman. We will take a taxi. — Biz taksi olamiz.

5. Va'da yoki taklif (Promise or offer)
I will call you tomorrow. — Men sizga ertaga qo'ng'iroq qilaman. She will help you with your homework. — U sizga uy vazifangizda yordam beradi. They will come to the party. — Ular ziyofatga kelishadi.
