Future Simple — Inkor shakl (Negative)

1. Kelajakda sodir bo'lmaydigan ish-harakat (Future action)
I will not visit my grandmother tomorrow. — Men ertaga buvimni ziyorat qilmayman. She will not cook dinner tonight. — U bugun kechqurun kechki ovqat pishirmaydi. They will not play football next week. — Ular keyingi hafta futbol o'ynamaydi.

2. Kelajakdagi holat (Future state)
He will not work at a bank. — U bankda ishlamaydi. We will not live in Tashkent. — Biz Toshkentda yashamaymiz. I will not study English. — Men ingliz tilini o'rganmayman.

3. Bashorat yoki taxmin (Prediction)
It will not rain tomorrow. — Ertaga yomg'ir yog'maydi. The team will not win the match. — Jamoa o'yinda g'alaba qozonmaydi. She will not be a great doctor. — U ajoyib shifokor bo'lmaydi.

4. O'sha paytda qabul qilingan qaror (Spontaneous decision)
I will not help you with that. — Men sizga bunda yordam bermayman. I will not answer the phone. — Men telefonga javob bermayman. We will not take a taxi. — Biz taksi olmaymiz.

5. Va'da yoki taklif (Promise or offer)
I will not call you tomorrow. — Men sizga ertaga qo'ng'iroq qilmayman. She will not help you with your homework. — U sizga uy vazifangizda yordam bermaydi. They will not come to the party. — Ular ziyofatga kelishmaydi.
