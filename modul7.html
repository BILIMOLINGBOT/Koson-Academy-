"Will be" — Bo'lishli shakl (Positive)

1. Holatni bildirish (State)
I will be happy. — Men baxtli bo'laman. She will be tired. — U charchagan bo'ladi. They will be ready. — Ular tayyor bo'ladi.

2. Kasb yoki maqom (Job / Status)
He will be a teacher. — U o'qituvchi bo'ladi. I will be a doctor. — Men shifokor bo'laman. We will be students. — Biz talaba bo'lamiz.

3. Yoshi (Age)
I will be 20 years old. — Men 20 yoshda bo'laman. She will be 25 years old. — U 25 yoshda bo'ladi. They will be adults. — Ular katta bo'ladi.

4. Qayerda ekanini aytish (Place)
I will be at home. — Men uyda bo'laman. He will be at work. — U ishda bo'ladi. We will be in Tashkent. — Biz Toshkentda bo'lamiz.

5. Sifat bilan gap (Adjective)
The weather will be warm. — Ob-havo iliq bo'ladi. My phone will be new. — Mening telefonim yangi bo'ladi. The exam will be difficult. — Imtihon qiyin bo'ladi.

6. Vaqt va sana (Time & Date)
It will be Monday tomorrow. — Ertaga dushanba bo'ladi. It will be 9 o'clock. — Soat 9 bo'ladi. It will be summer soon. — Tez orada yoz bo'ladi.
