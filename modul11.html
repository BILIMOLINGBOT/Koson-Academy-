Present Continuous — So'roq shakl (Question)

1. Hozir bajarilayotgan ish-harakat (Action happening now)
Am I reading a book? — Men kitob o'qiyapmanmi? Is she cooking dinner? — U kechki ovqat pishiryaptimi? Are they playing football? — Ular futbol o'ynayaptimi?

2. Hozirgi paytdagi holat (Current situation)
Is he working at a bank? — U bankda ishlayaptimi? Are we living in Tashkent? — Biz Toshkentda yashayapmizmi? Am I studying English? — Men ingliz tilini o'rganyapmanmi?

3. Rejalashtirilgan kelajak (Planned future)
Am I meeting my friend tomorrow? — Men ertaga do'stim bilan uchrashyapmanmi? Is she traveling to Paris next week? — U keyingi hafta Parijga ketyaptimi? Are we having a party on Saturday? — Biz shanba kuni ziyofat o'tkazyapmizmi?

4. Vaqtinchalik holat (Temporary situation)
Is he staying at a hotel? — U mehmonxonada turibdimi? Am I taking a course this month? — Men bu oy kurs o'qiyapmanmi? Is she working from home today? — U bugun uydan ishlayaptimi?

5. O'zgarayotgan vaziyat (Changing situation)
Is the weather getting warmer? — Ob-havo iliqlashyaptimi? Are prices increasing? — Narxlar oshyaptimi? Is my English improving? — Mening ingliz tilim yaxshilanyaptimi?
