"Was" va "Were" — Inkor shakl (Negative)

1. Holatni bildirish (State)
I was not tired. — Men charchagan emas edim. She was not happy. — U xursand emas edi. They were not ready. — Ular tayyor emas edi.

2. Kasb yoki maqom (Job / Status)
He was not a teacher. — U o'qituvchi emas edi. I was not a student. — Men talaba emas edim. We were not doctors. — Biz shifokor emas edik.

3. Yoshi (Age)
I was not 20 years old. — Men 20 yoshda emas edim. She was not 15 years old. — U 15 yoshda emas edi. They were not children. — Ular bola emas edi.

4. Qayerda ekanini aytish (Place)
I was not at home. — Men uyda emas edim. He was not at work. — U ishda emas edi. We were not in Tashkent. — Biz Toshkentda emas edik.

5. Sifat bilan gap (Adjective)
The weather was not cold. — Ob-havo sovuq emas edi. My phone was not new. — Mening telefonim yangi emas edi. The movie was not interesting. — Kino qiziqarli emas edi.

6. Vaqt va sana (Time & Date)
It was not Monday. — Dushanba emas edi. It was not 9 o'clock. — Soat 9 emas edi. It was not summer. — Yoz emas edi.
