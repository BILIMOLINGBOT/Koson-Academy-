To be (am / is / are) — ingliz tilida eng muhim yordamchi fe’llardan biri. O‘zbekchadagi “bo‘lmoq”, ba’zan esa “-man, -san, -dir” ma’nolarini beradi. Qachon to be ishlatamiz? A) Kim yoki nima ekanligini aytganda I am a student. Men talabaman. He is a teacher. U o‘qituvchi. Formula: Ega + am/is/are + kasb/kim/nima B) Sifat bilan I am happy. — Men xursandman. She is tired. — U charchagan. They are busy. — Ular band. Formula: Ega + am/is/are + sifat C) Joylashuvni aytganda I am at home. — Men uydaman. He is at school. — U maktabda. They are in Tashkent. — Ular Toshkentda. Formula: Ega + am/is/are + joy
