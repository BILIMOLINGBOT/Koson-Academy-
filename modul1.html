"To be" fe'lining bo'lishli shakli (Positive)

1. Holatni bildirish (State)
I am happy. — Men baxtliman. She is tired. — U charchagan. We are ready. — Biz tayyormiz.

2. Kasb yoki maqom (Job / Status)
I am a doctor. — Men shifokorman. He is a teacher. — U o'qituvchi. They are students. — Ular talabalar.

3. Yoshi (Age)
I am 25 years old. — Men 25 yoshdaman. She is 18 years old. — U 18 yoshda. We are 30 years old. — Biz 30 yoshdamiz.

4. Qayerda ekanini aytish (Place)
I am at home. — Men uydaman. He is at work. — U ishda. They are in Tashkent. — Ular Toshkentda.

5. Sifat bilan gap (Adjective)
The weather is warm. — Ob-havo iliq. My phone is new. — Mening telefonim yangi. The book is interesting. — Kitob qiziqarli.

6. Millat yoki kelib chiqish (Nationality)
I am Uzbek. — Men o'zbekman. She is Russian. — U rus. They are American. — Ular amerikalik.

7. Vaqt va sana (Time & Date)
It is Monday. — Bugun dushanba. It is 9 o'clock. — Soat 9. It is summer. — Hozir yoz.
