O'zingizni tanishtiring

Hello! What is your name?
Salom! Ismingiz nima?
My name is Ali. What is your name?
Mening ismim Ali. Sizning ismingiz nima?
My name is Madina. Nice to meet you.
Mening ismim Madina. Tanishganimdan xursandman.
Nice to meet you too.
Men ham tanishganimdan xursandman.
How old are you?
Siz necha yoshdasiz?
I am 18 years old.
Men 18 yoshdaman.
Where are you from?
Siz qayerdansiz?
I am from Uzbekistan.
Men Oʻzbekistondanman.
Which city do you live in?
Siz qaysi shaharda yashaysiz?
I live in Tashkent.
Men Toshkentda yashayman.
Are you a student?
Siz talabamisiz?
Yes, I am a student.
Ha, men talabaman.
What do you study?
Siz nima oʻqiysiz?
I study English.
Men ingliz tilini oʻrganaman.
Do you work?
Siz ishlaysizmi?
No, I do not work now.
Yoʻq, hozir ishlamayman.
What do you like to do?
Siz nima qilishni yoqtirasiz?
I like reading books.
Men kitob oʻqishni yoqtiraman.
Do you have brothers or sisters?
Sizning aka-ukalaringiz yoki opa-singillaringiz bormi?
Yes, I have one brother.
Ha, mening bitta ukam bor.
What is your favorite food?
Sizning sevimli ovqatingiz nima?
My favorite food is plov.
Mening sevimli ovqatim palov.
What is your favorite color?
Sizning sevimli rangingiz nima?
My favorite color is blue.
Mening sevimli rangim koʻk.
Can you speak English well?
Siz ingliz tilida yaxshi gaplasha olasizmi?
I can speak a little English.
Men ozgina inglizcha gaplasha olaman.
What do you do every day?
Siz har kuni nima qilasiz?
I go to school every day.
Men har kuni maktabga boraman.
What are you doing now?
Hozir nima qilyapsiz?
I am learning English now.
Men hozir ingliz tilini oʻrganayapman.
What did you do yesterday?
Kecha nima qildingiz?
I watched a movie yesterday.
Men kecha kino koʻrdim.
Were you at home yesterday evening?
Kecha kechqurun uyda edingizmi?
Yes, I was at home.
Ha, men uyda edim.
Do you have a phone?
Sizning telefoningiz bormi?
Yes, I have a smartphone.
Ha, mening smartfonim bor.
What is your dream?
Sizning orzuingiz nima?
My dream is to speak English fluently.
Mening orzuim ingliz tilida ravon gapirish.
Thank you for talking with me.
Men bilan suhbatlashganingiz uchun rahmat.
You are welcome. See you later!
Arzimaydi. Keyin koʻrishamiz!
