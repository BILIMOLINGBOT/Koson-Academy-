"Have got" va "Have" — Bo'lishli shakl (Positive)

1. Oil a'zolari (Family members)
I have got a brother. — Mening akam bor. She has got two sisters. — Uning ikkita opasi bor. I have a big family. — Mening katta oilam bor.

2. Shaxsiy narsalar (Personal possessions)
I have got a new phone. — Mening yangi telefonim bor. He has got a car. — Uning mashinasi bor. We have a house in Tashkent. — Bizning Toshkentda uyimiz bor.

3. Tashqi ko'rinish (Physical appearance)
She has got long hair. — Uning sochlari uzun. He has got blue eyes. — Uning ko'zlari ko'k. I have short hair. — Mening sochlarim kalta.

4. Kasallik yoki holat (Illness / Condition)
I have got a headache. — Mening boshim og'riyapti. She has got a cold. — U shamollagan. He has a fever. — Uning isitmasi bor.

5. Imkoniyat yoki reja (Opportunity / Plan)
I have got a meeting today. — Bugun mening uchrashuvim bor. We have got tickets for the concert. — Bizda konsertga chiptalar bor. She has an exam tomorrow. — Uning ertaga imtihoni bor.

6. Hayvonlar (Animals / Pets)
I have got a cat. — Mening mushugim bor. They have got a dog. — Ularning iti bor. He has a parrot. — Uning to'tisi bor.
