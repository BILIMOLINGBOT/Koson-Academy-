Future Continuous — So'roq shakl (Question)

1. Kelajakda ma'lum paytda davom etayotgan ish (Action in progress at a specific time in the future)
Will I be reading a book at 8 PM? — Men soat 8 da kitob o'qiyotgan bo'lamanmi? Will she be cooking dinner tomorrow? — U ertaga kechki ovqat pishirayotgan bo'ladimi? Will they be playing football this time next week? — Keyingi hafta shu paytda ular futbol o'ynayotgan bo'ladimi?

2. Kelajakda davom etadigan holat (Continuous state in the future)
Will he be working at a bank next year? — U keyingi yil bankda ishlayotgan bo'ladimi? Will we be living in Tashkent next month? — Biz keyingi oy Toshkentda yashayotgan bo'lamizmi? Will I be studying English this evening? — Men bugun kechqurun ingliz tilini o'rganayotgan bo'lamanmi?

3. Kelajakda davom etayotgan vaqtinchalik holat (Temporary situation in the future)
Will he be staying at a hotel next week? — U keyingi hafta mehmonxonada turgan bo'ladimi? Will I be taking a course this summer? — Men bu yoz kurs o'qiyotgan bo'lamanmi? Will she be working from home tomorrow? — U ertaga uydan ishlayotgan bo'ladimi?

4. Kelajakdagi rejalashtirilgan davomli ish (Planned continuous action)
Will we be traveling to Paris next month? — Biz keyingi oy Parijga sayohat qilayotgan bo'lamizmi? Will they be having a meeting at 10 AM? — Ular soat 10 da yig'ilish o'tkazayotgan bo'ladimi? Will I be waiting for you at the station? — Men sizni stansiyada kutayotgan bo'lamanmi?

5. Odatiy kelajakdagi davomli ish (Routine continuous action in the future)
Will I be working all day tomorrow? — Men ertaga kun bo'yi ishlayotgan bo'lamanmi? Will she be studying for the exam next week? — U keyingi hafta imtihonga tayyorlanayotgan bo'ladimi? Will they be building a new school? — Ular yangi maktab qurayotgan bo'ladimi?
