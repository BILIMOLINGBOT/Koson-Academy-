Past Simple — Bo'lishli shakl (Positive)

1. O'tgan zamonda tugallangan ish-harakat (Completed action in the past)
I visited my grandmother yesterday. — Men kecha buvimni ziyorat qildim. She cooked dinner last night. — U kecha kechki ovqat pishirdi. They played football last week. — Ular o'tgan hafta futbol o'ynadi.

2. O'tgan zamondagi holat (State in the past)
He worked at a bank. — U bankda ishlagan. We lived in Tashkent. — Biz Toshkentda yashaganmiz. I studied English at school. — Men maktabda ingliz tilini o'rganganman.

3. Ketma-ket sodir bo'lgan ish-harakatlar (Sequence of actions)
I woke up, got dressed, and left the house. — Men uyg'ondim, kiyindim va uydan chiqdim. She finished her homework and watched TV. — U uy vazifasini tugatdi va televizor ko'rdi. They arrived, sat down, and ordered food. — Ular kelishdi, o'tirishdi va ovqat buyurtma qilishdi.

4. O'tgan zamondagi odat (Habit in the past)
I walked to school every day. — Men har kuni maktabga piyoda borardim. She drank tea every morning. — U har kuni ertalab choy ichardi. They visited us often. — Ular bizga tez-tez tashrif buyurishardi.

5. Aniq vaqtda sodir bo'lgan ish (Action at a specific time)
I met him last Monday. — Men u bilan o'tgan dushanba kuni uchrashdim. She called me at 5 PM. — U menga soat 5 da qo'ng'iroq qildi. They moved here in 2020. — Ular bu yerga 2020-yilda ko'chib kelishdi.
