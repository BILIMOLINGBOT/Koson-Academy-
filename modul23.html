Past Simple — So'roq shakl (Question)

1. O'tgan zamonda tugallangan ish-harakat (Completed action in the past)
Did I visit my grandmother yesterday? — Men kecha buvimni ziyorat qildimmi? Did she cook dinner last night? — U kecha kechki ovqat pishirdimi? Did they play football last week? — Ular o'tgan hafta futbol o'ynadimi?

2. O'tgan zamondagi holat (State in the past)
Did he work at a bank? — U bankda ishlaganmi? Did we live in Tashkent? — Biz Toshkentda yashaganmizmi? Did I study English at school? — Men maktabda ingliz tilini o'rganganmanmi?

3. Ketma-ket sodir bo'lgan ish-harakatlar (Sequence of actions)
Did I wake up, get dressed, and leave the house? — Men uyg'ondim, kiyindim va uydan chiqdimmi? Did she finish her homework and watch TV? — U uy vazifasini tugatdi va televizor ko'rdimi? Did they arrive, sit down, and order food? — Ular kelishdi, o'tirishdi va ovqat buyurtma qilishdimi?

4. O'tgan zamondagi odat (Habit in the past)
Did I walk to school every day? — Men har kuni maktabga piyoda borardimmi? Did she drink tea every morning? — U har kuni ertalab choy ichardimi? Did they visit us often? — Ular bizga tez-tez tashrif buyurishardimi?

5. Aniq vaqtda sodir bo'lgan ish (Action at a specific time)
Did I meet him last Monday? — Men u bilan o'tgan dushanba kuni uchrashdimmi? Did she call me at 5 PM? — U menga soat 5 da qo'ng'iroq qildimi? Did they move here in 2020? — Ular bu yerga 2020-yilda ko'chib kelishdimi?
